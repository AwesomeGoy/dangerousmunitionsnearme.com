Privacy

Dangerous Munitions Near Me is a site by D.A. Williams. It is not an official U.S. government document.

What the site does in your browser

The map, site pages, ZIP search, timeline, and downloads run in your browser. Those tools do not send your ZIP code, location, or search terms to a server operated by this site.

The site is hosted as static files. The host and your internet provider may keep ordinary technical logs (such as an IP address and the page requested), as most hosts do. This site does not use those logs to identify a person and does not sell them.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to count visits and see which pages are used. Google sets cookies and receives information such as the pages you view, your approximate location, and your device and browser. Names and email addresses are not sent to Google Analytics.

You can block this with your browser settings or with Google’s opt-out add-on. Google explains how it uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

ZIP alerts

The “Get alerts for this ZIP” signup lets you ask for email updates when this site records a documented incident, permit change, or public-comment deadline at a tracked munitions site, or a status change at a Formerly Used Defense Site, an EPA Superfund, USACE FUSRAP, DOE Legacy Management, Navajo-area abandoned uranium mine, mapped military PFAS or mapped sea-dump site, within 50 miles of a U.S. ZIP code you enter, and changes to military PFAS and sea-dump sites that have no map point, in or off the state that ZIP code belongs to. Distance is measured in a straight line from the ZIP’s Census ZCTA centroid to the site coordinates and is not a measure of risk.

When you sign up, the only information collected is:

  • The email address you submit
  • The five-digit U.S. ZIP code you want alerts for
  • The time of your signup and confirmation, so we can show that you asked for the messages
  • Standard email-delivery records created by the mailing service (for example, whether a message bounced or you unsubscribed)

A hidden site marker (dmnm) may also be stored so these signups can be told apart from other lists on the same mailing account. We do not ask for your name, street address, phone number, or precise location. The “Use my location” map tool, if you use it, stays in your browser and is not used for alerts.

How that information is used

  • To send the alerts you requested for the ZIP code you entered, only when a documented change is recorded at a tracked munitions, FUDS munitions, Superfund, FUSRAP, DOE Legacy Management, Navajo-area abandoned uranium mine, mapped military PFAS or mapped sea-dump site within 50 miles of that ZIP, or at an unmapped military PFAS or sea-dump site in or off that ZIP’s state
  • To confirm your email address before any alerts are sent (double opt-in)
  • To honor unsubscribe requests and keep a suppression record so you are not emailed again unless you re-subscribe
  • To fix delivery problems

We do not sell the list, use it for advertising, or share it for others to use. We do not send routine newsletters, fundraising, or unrelated book or site promotions through this list. MailerLite is the email service used to store the list and send the messages. That service sees the email address and ZIP code in order to deliver the mail.

Your choices

  • You have to confirm the signup from your email inbox before any alerts are sent
  • Every alert includes a way to unsubscribe
  • You can also write to D.A. Williams through awesomegoy.com and ask to be removed or to see what is on file for that email address

Neighbor stories

The Neighbor stories form lets an adult send a first-person account about a tracked site. A person reads every submission before anything is posted. Nothing is published automatically.

The form asks for some of the following, all typed by the submitter:

  • A story or statement, and the site it relates to
  • An optional display name (first name, initials, or “name withheld”)
  • Optional town or ZIP, only as the submitter wants it shown
  • A contact email, used only to follow up about the submission, not published
  • Confirmation that the submitter is 18 or older and that the story is their own account

No payment information is collected. The form does not ask for a home address, phone number, or official ID.

How story submissions are used

  • The story and the chosen display name or town may be published if the submission is accepted under the moderation policy.
  • The contact email is used only to ask a clarifying question, to confirm a redaction, or to notify the submitter of a decision. It is not sold, given to advertisers, or listed on the site.
  • Unpublished submissions and contact emails are kept only as long as needed to review the story and handle a later correction or takedown request, then deleted.

Only the approved story text, the site it refers to, the date, and the display name or “name withheld” are published. Contact email is not published. Exact home addresses and phone numbers are removed if someone includes them. Submissions from people under 18 are not accepted.

Form delivery

The stories form is delivered by FormSubmit, a free form-to-email service. FormSubmit’s privacy policy is at formsubmit.co/privacy.pdf. That service receives what you type in the form so it can be emailed to D.A. Williams for review. FormSubmit may keep a copy for up to 30 days to deliver the message. We do not use FormSubmit for advertising.

Requests about a story

You can write to D.A. Williams through awesomegoy.com and ask for a copy, a correction, or deletion of a submission you sent, or a correction of a specific factual error in a published story that clearly identifies you.

Email alerts (location and radius)

The email-alerts signup lets you ask for updates when this site records a documented blast, evacuation, or official cleanup update at a tracked site within a distance you choose of a location you save. Distance is measured in a straight line and is not a measure of risk.

When you sign up, the information collected is:

  • The email address you submit
  • A five-digit U.S. ZIP code, if you enter one
  • A location description, if you enter one
  • A map point (latitude and longitude), rounded to two decimal places (about 1 km), if you share one from this device or type it
  • The alert radius you choose
  • The time of your signup and confirmation, so we can show that you asked for the messages
  • Standard email-delivery records created by the mailing service (for example, whether a message bounced or you unsubscribed)

A hidden site marker (dmnm) may also be stored so these signups can be told apart from other lists on the same mailing account. We do not ask for your name, street address, or phone number. Device location is used only if you press “Use my location,” and only to fill the map point on the form. We do not watch your location in the background, and we do not look up or guess coordinates from a place name. Your email address and location fields are never shown on this site or published anywhere.

How alert information is used

  • To send the alerts you requested, only when a documented change is recorded at a tracked site within the radius you chose of the location you saved
  • To confirm your email address before any alerts are sent (double opt-in)
  • To honor unsubscribe requests and keep a suppression record so you are not emailed again unless you re-subscribe
  • To fix delivery problems

We do not sell the list, use it for advertising, or share it for others to use. We do not send routine newsletters, fundraising, or unrelated book or site promotions through this list. MailerLite is the email service used to store the list and send the messages. That service sees the email address and the location fields you submitted in order to deliver the mail. Every alert includes a way to unsubscribe.

Report-a-site tips

The “Report a site” form lets anyone send a tip about a place that may belong on this map. A person reads every tip. Tips are checked against official or primary sources. Nothing is published automatically. A tip is not by itself a source.

The form asks for some of the following, all typed by the submitter:

  • The place the tip is about
  • What should be listed, and when, if known
  • Links to official or primary sources, if the submitter has them
  • An optional contact email and name, used only to follow up about the tip, not published

How tips are used

  • To investigate whether an official or primary source supports adding or changing a record
  • The optional contact email is used only to ask a clarifying question. It is not sold, given to advertisers, listed on the site, or added to the alerts list
  • Unpublished tips and contact emails are kept only as long as needed to review the tip and handle a later correction, then deleted

The tip form is delivered by FormSubmit, a free form-to-email service, to a private inbox. FormSubmit’s privacy policy is at formsubmit.co/privacy.pdf. That service receives what you type so it can be emailed for review. FormSubmit may keep a copy for up to 30 days to deliver the message. We do not use FormSubmit for advertising. Tips are never posted to a public GitHub issue or other public queue. When a tip is sent, FormSubmit may show a reCAPTCHA check (a Google service) on its own page to block spam.
