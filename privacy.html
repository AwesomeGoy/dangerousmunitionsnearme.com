Privacy notice for ZIP alerts

Dangerous Munitions Near Me is a site by D.A. Williams. It is not an official U.S. government document.

What the site does in your browser

The map, site pages, ZIP search, timeline, and downloads run in your browser. Those tools do not send your ZIP code, location, or search terms to a server operated by this site.

The site is hosted as static files. The host and your internet provider may keep ordinary technical logs (such as an IP address and the page requested), as most hosts do. This site does not use those logs to identify a person and does not sell them.

ZIP alerts

The “Get alerts for this ZIP” signup lets you ask for email updates when this site records a documented incident, permit change, or public-comment deadline at a tracked site within 50 miles of a U.S. ZIP code you enter. Distance is measured in a straight line from the ZIP’s Census ZCTA centroid to the site coordinates and is not a measure of risk.

When you sign up, the only information collected is:

  • The email address you submit
  • The five-digit U.S. ZIP code you want alerts for
  • The time of your signup and confirmation, so we can show that you asked for the messages
  • Standard email-delivery records created by the mailing service (for example, whether a message bounced or you unsubscribed)

A hidden site marker (dmnm) may also be stored so these signups can be told apart from other lists on the same mailing account. We do not ask for your name, street address, phone number, or precise location. The “Use my location” map tool, if you use it, stays in your browser and is not used for alerts.

How that information is used

  • To send the alerts you requested for the ZIP code you entered, only when a documented change is recorded at a tracked site within 50 miles of that ZIP
  • To confirm your email address before any alerts are sent (double opt-in)
  • To honor unsubscribe requests and keep a suppression record so you are not emailed again unless you re-subscribe
  • To fix delivery problems

We do not sell the list, use it for advertising, or share it for others to use. We do not send routine newsletters, fundraising, or unrelated book or site promotions through this list. MailerLite is the email service used to store the list and send the messages. That service sees the email address and ZIP code in order to deliver the mail.

Your choices

  • You have to confirm the signup from your email inbox before any alerts are sent
  • Every alert includes a way to unsubscribe
  • You can also write to D.A. Williams through awesomegoy.com and ask to be removed or to see what is on file for that email address

Neighbor stories

The neighbor-stories form is still a preview only. It is disabled and does not collect or store submissions.
