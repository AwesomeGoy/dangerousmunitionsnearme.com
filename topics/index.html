Topics

Worldwide topic pages that cut across the country and region sections: each collects one kind of hazard, links every record to its sources and marks it verified, corrected or unverified.

These pages are not official government documents and do not say any place is safe or unsafe. ZIP alerts on this site cover U.S. sites only and do not include topic-page records.

  • Petroleum explosions, spills and contamination

    Major petroleum explosions, oil spills and long-running oil contamination worldwide, from official and primary sources. 71 records (58 verified, 5 corrected, 8 unverified); 42 mapped.

  • Nuclear test sites

    Where nuclear weapons were tested, by testing state: test counts, largest yields, contamination and compensation programs, from official sources. 35 records (23 verified, 0 corrected, 12 unverified); 12 mapped.

  • Chemical weapons and sea dumps

    Chemical weapons stockpiles and their destruction, wartime and post-war sea dumps, and notable chemical weapons incidents, from OPCW, U.S. Army, HELCOM and national records. 63 records (58 verified, 1 corrected, 4 unverified); 14 mapped.

  • Chemical-plant disasters

    Major chemical-plant and chemical-storage explosions and toxic releases worldwide, from official investigation reports. 14 records (13 verified, 0 corrected, 1 unverified); 3 mapped.

  • Nuclear accidents and waste

    Nuclear and radiological accidents, nuclear-waste repositories and legacy contamination worldwide, from regulators, the IAEA and national agencies. 37 records (31 verified, 2 corrected, 4 unverified); 8 mapped.

  • Firing and bombing ranges

    Military firing, bombing and weapons-test ranges with documented contamination, unexploded ordnance or civilian incidents. 16 records (14 verified, 0 corrected, 2 unverified); 7 mapped.

  • Wartime unexploded ordnance

    Countries and areas still contaminated by landmines, cluster munitions and other unexploded wartime ordnance, with official figures where published. 14 records (11 verified, 1 corrected, 2 unverified); 0 mapped.

  • Coal-ash and tailings dam failures

    Failures of coal-ash, coal-refuse and mine-tailings dams and impoundments, with release volumes and casualties from official investigations. 12 records (9 verified, 2 corrected, 1 unverified); 4 mapped.
