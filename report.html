About language on this site

The menus, labels and disclaimers are shown in the language you choose. Record names, research notes and quotations stay in English. Source titles stay in their original language. They are not machine-translated.

Report a site

If you know of a blast, evacuation, cleanup, contamination site, or munitions hazard that should be on this map, you can send a tip. A person checks every tip against official or primary sources. Nothing is published automatically, and a tip is not by itself a source.

Please include links to official or primary sources (government, court, treaty body, company filings, or the operator). This site does not use The New York Times, CNN, MSNBC or MS NOW, Fox News, Snopes, or PolitiFact as sources.

Tips go to a private inbox for review. They are not posted to the site, to social media, or to a public issue tracker. See the privacy notice for how FormSubmit delivers the message and how long a copy may be kept.
