About language on this site

The menus, labels and disclaimers are shown in the language you choose. Record names, research notes and quotations stay in English. Source titles stay in their original language. They are not machine-translated.

What's near me? Worldwide hazard lookup

Type a place, address, ZIP or postal code, or use your device location. We list every mapped hazard record in this database within the distance you choose, nearest first.

What happens to the place you type

When you press Search, the place, address, ZIP or postal code you typed is sent to a third-party place-search service to turn it into coordinates: OpenStreetMap Nominatim (run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation), and Photon (run by Komoot) if Nominatim does not answer. Those services receive what you typed and your internet (IP) address, under their own privacy policies. This site does not store, log or share what you type or your location, does not keep the search replies, and never puts your location in the page address or in analytics. If you use “Use my location” instead, your device location stays in your browser and nothing is sent to those services.

The map is off until you open it. When you open it, map pictures for the area shown are loaded from the OpenStreetMap tile servers, which can see which area is being viewed.

Examples: Phoenix, AZ · Middletown, Iowa · B3H 4R2 · Kyiv
