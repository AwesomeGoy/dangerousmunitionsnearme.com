Chemical-plant disasters

Major chemical-plant and chemical-storage explosions and toxic releases worldwide, from official investigation reports. 14 records: 13 verified, 0 corrected against a better official source and 1 unverified and marked as such.

This page is not an official government document and does not say any place is safe or unsafe. It is a selection of documented places, not a complete list. ZIP alerts on this site do not include these records.

Read before using this page

  • 1 records are unverified and labeled in yellow: Union Carbide India Ltd pesticide plant methyl isocyanate leak, Bhopal. Figures in them are reported, not confirmed in an official source.
  • Death and injury tolls are those printed by the official investigation. For Bhopal no official toll was confirmed in an opened official document, so none is given.
  • Petroleum refinery and fuel-depot disasters are on the Petroleum page and are cross-referenced below, not repeated.
  • Blank coordinates mean list only. Nothing was geocoded or guessed.

3 records are mapped as points. Unverified records have a dashed yellow outline. The other 11 records have no usable official coordinate and are listed below only.

Chemical-plant disasters (14; 3 mapped)

  • Union Carbide India Ltd pesticide plant methyl isocyanate leak, Bhopal — Bhopal, Madhya Pradesh, IND Unverified
    Date: 1984-12-02/03 (night)
    Event: toxic gas release
    Substance: methyl isocyanate (MIC)
    Operator: Union Carbide India Limited (UCIL)
    Other impacts: MP BGTRRD: 10,29,519 compensation claims registered; 5,74,393 cases awarded, 4,55,126 rejected; Rs 3,066.63 crore compensation awarded; Rs 861.55 crore ex-gratia.
    What changed afterwards: State relief and rehabilitation department (BGTRRD) still operating.
    Unverified: Claims and compensation figures were read on the Madhya Pradesh government page. No death toll is given here: the commonly quoted “5,295 compensated deaths” (Group of Ministers, June 2010) was seen only on an NGO site and in a news-agency quote, not on an opened official page.
    List only: no official coordinate was found, so it is not on the map (not geocoded). Checked 2026-11-25.
    Sources: Government of Madhya Pradesh, Bhopal Gas Tragedy Relief & Rehabilitation Department, 'Facts and Figures'
  • Tianjin Port Ruihai International Logistics hazardous-goods warehouse fire and explosions — Binhai New Area, Tianjin Port, Tianjin, CHN Verified
    Date: 2015-08-12
    Event: fire then explosions in a hazardous-goods yard
    Substance: nitrocellulose (self-ignited), ammonium nitrate (~800 t exploded), sodium cyanide and other hazardous goods
    Operator: Tianjin Dongjiang Port Ruihai International Logistics Co.
    Deaths: 165 dead (incl. 110 firefighters and police) + 8 missing
    Injuries: 798 injured
    Other impacts: 304 buildings, 12,428 vehicles and 7,533 containers damaged; direct economic loss 6.866 billion yuan. About 3% of the used sodium cyanide remained in soil after clean-up; no deaths attributed to pollution.
    Point is the explosion centre printed in the official investigation report. Checked 2026-11-25.
    Sources: State Council investigation team, 'Investigation report on the 8.12 Tianjin Port Ruihai Company hazardous goods warehouse fire and… · Xinhua (state news agency), summary of the State Council Tianjin investigation report, 5 Feb 2016
  • AZF (Grande Paroisse) fertilizer plant explosion, Toulouse — Toulouse, Occitanie (Haute-Garonne), FRA Verified
    Date: 2001-09-21 10:17
    Event: explosion of used ammonium nitrate
    Substance: ammonium nitrate off-spec rejects, 20-120 t in hangar 221 (20-40 t TNT equivalent estimated)
    Operator: Grande Paroisse (TotalFinaElf group)
    Deaths: 31 (21 on site, 1 at the neighbouring plant, 9 outside)
    Injuries: 2,442 (prefecture figure of 17 Oct 2001)
    Other impacts: Crater about 65 x 45 x 7 m; widespread damage across Toulouse. 2006 court experts: mixing of sodium dichloroisocyanurate (DCCNa) with ammonium nitrate as cause.
    List only: no official coordinate was found, so it is not on the map (not geocoded). Checked 2026-11-25.
    Sources: French Ministry of Ecological Transition, BARPI/ARIA database, accident no. 21329 (AZF Toulouse), detailed sheet and PDF
