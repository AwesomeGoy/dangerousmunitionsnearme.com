Australia and New Zealand

Official Australian and New Zealand records of areas affected by unexploded ordnance (UXO), military PFAS sites, ammunition sea-dumping areas and other munitions records, plus links to contaminated-land registers. Every record is from a government source and is labeled partly verified. Blank means no official value was found.

This page is not an official government document and does not say any place is safe or unsafe. In Australia, if you find something that may be UXO, don’t touch it; call 000. In New Zealand, call 111. ZIP alerts on this site cover U.S. sites only; Australia and New Zealand records are not part of alerts.

Australian UXO points are the official map’s centre points, not the area outlines. PFAS points are base locations, not where contamination was found.

New Zealand: NZDF PFAS sites (8)

From the 2018 Cabinet paper, NZDF investigation reports and later official statements. 5 have a map point from the LINZ NZ Gazetteer, which is the base location, not the contamination location. NZDF has no public PFAS page, so for some bases the newest information is RNZ reporting of NZDF statements; this is marked on those records.

  • RNZAF Base Ohakea — Manawatū-Whanganui
    Long-term monitoring (rounds Oct 2020 to early 2025) "shows very little long-term change" (NZDF evidence, Apr 2025). Nov 2023 round: 7 bores above drinking-water standard, concentrations within historical ranges (per RNZ report of the monitoring report). Ōhakea Rural Water Scheme opened 2022, serving the base and 90+ households/farms, 7 of them PFAS-affected. Horizons reviewed PFAS monitoring near Makowhai Stream in 2025-26.
    List only, no official map point. Data date: 2025-04-29 (NZDF evidence); 2026-10-28 (Horizons draft annual report). Includes RNZ reporting of NZDF statements.
    Sources: Waitangi Tribunal Wai 2200, NZDF brief of evidence (Robert Owen), 29 April 2025 · Horizons Regional Council draft Annual Report 2025-26 (28 Oct 2026): review of PFAS monito · RNZ, 17 July 2024: High levels persist at Ohakea (reporting the Nov 2023 NZDF/Horizons mon · NZ Cabinet paper: All of Government PFAS programme (overview as at 26 April 2018)
  • RNZAF Base Woodbourne — Marlborough
    Ongoing NZDF long-term monitoring. One eastern-edge bore over 4x the PFHxS+PFOS guideline in 2023 and June 2024; most samples below guidelines (NZDF via RNZ, Oct 2024). 2017-18 investigation: plume about 200 ha, 5 off-site samples over the 70 ng/L standard (ESR/EPA 2023 summary). Oct/Nov 2024 Crown paid Kurahaupō iwi $25.2m because contamination stopped purchase of remaining base land.
    Map point is the base location, not the contamination location. Data date: 2024-06 (latest reported sampling); 2024-11 (settlement). Includes RNZ reporting of NZDF statements.
