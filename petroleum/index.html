Petroleum: explosions, spills and contamination

A worldwide set of 71 petroleum sites with major explosions, oil spills or long-running contamination, from official and primary sources: 26 refinery, terminal, pipeline, platform and rail explosions and fires, 28 major oil spills, and 17 long-running contamination sites, including 10 refinery sites on the U.S. Superfund National Priorities List. Of the 71 records, 58 are verified, 5 were corrected against a better official source, and 8 are unverified and marked as such.

This page is not an official government document and does not say any place is safe or unsafe. It is a selection of major events, not a complete list. ZIP alerts on this site do not include these petroleum records.

Read before using the map

  • 8 records are unverified and labeled in yellow: Jesse pipeline fire (Nigeria, 1998), San Juanico LPG explosions (Mexico, 1984), Tlahuelilpan pipeline explosion (Mexico, 2019), Matanzas fuel depot fire (Cuba, 2022), Jaipur oil terminal fire (India, 2009), Norilsk diesel spill (Russia, 2020), Penglai 19-3 oil field leaks (China, 2011), Lago Agrio oil fields (Ecuador). Their death tolls or volumes are reported, not confirmed in an official source.
  • Figures are quoted as each official source gives them. Where official sources disagree, both figures are shown. Figures read only from a summary because the source document could not be opened are marked “reported”.
  • The 1991 Gulf War marker is regional. NOAA’s point off Kuwait marks the region of the releases, not a release site.
  • Kirtland’s point marks the air base, not the Bulk Fuels Facility where the leak occurred.
  • The 10 Superfund refinery records carry EPA listing data only (listing dates, Hazard Ranking System score and status), with links to EPA’s site profiles. Many petroleum-only sites are not on the Superfund list because the Superfund law (CERCLA) excludes petroleum from its definition of hazardous substances.
  • Points are incident- or facility-level. They come only from NOAA IncidentNews incident points, EPA Superfund site points and EPA facility registry points. NOAA points are often approximate.
  • Blank coordinates mean list only. 29 records have no usable official coordinate and are listed below but not mapped. Nothing was geocoded or guessed.

42 records are mapped. Unverified records have a dashed yellow outline; the Gulf War regional marker is drawn as a dashed ring. The Deepwater Horizon explosion and spill share one point.
