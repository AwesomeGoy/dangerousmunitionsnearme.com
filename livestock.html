Poultry, cattle and hog operations (United States)

A layer of U.S. poultry, cattle and hog operations and related official records: NPDES permits in EPA’s ICIS-NPDES system, documented NPDES enforcement or single-event violations, Iowa’s official animal-feeding-operation map, official state agency lists for nine more states, waters that states officially list as impaired with an animal-feeding or grazing probable source (EPA ATTAINS), and USDA APHIS confirmed HPAI / H5N1 detections (county or state only). By D.A. Williams. Checked 2026-11-26.

A permitted operation is not by itself proof of contamination. Permitted large operations, documented NPDES enforcement or single-event violations, Iowa DNR and other state agency listings, impaired-water listings, and USDA disease detections are shown as separate filters. A water listed as impaired with an animal-feeding probable source is a state assessment of that water, not a finding about any particular farm. This is not a complete national inventory. EPA withdrew a proposed CAFO reporting rule in 2012 and does not publish a complete list of concentrated animal feeding operations. Living near a listed operation does not necessarily mean a person is at risk. This page is not medical or legal advice.

Hogs (swine)

Swine is taken only from official codes and fields, never from facility names: ICIS-NPDES SIC 0213 (Hogs) or NAICS 112210 (Hog and Pig Farming); the Iowa DNR swine animal-unit field; and each state layer’s own animal type or per-species counts. National NPDES: 1,811 records carry a swine code (1,572 mapped); 1,680 of them are swine only (1,342 permits, 338 with documented enforcement) and 131 also list cattle or poultry. Iowa DNR: 10,080 sites report swine (9,425 swine only). State lists: 7,819 records report swine (North Carolina 2,051, Minnesota 4,250, Indiana 1,302, Missouri 213, Michigan 3). The Hogs (swine) filter is off by default; Only operations that list swine shows every record with a swine code, whatever else it lists.

Waters listed as impaired with an animal-feeding or grazing probable source

These are assessment units that a state, territory or tribe reported to EPA as impaired (Clean Water Act section 303(d) list, category 5, 5A or 5R, or impaired with a plan or other control in place, category 4A, 4B or 4C, overall status “Not Supporting”), where the listed probable source of at least one cause is one of the categories below. Only each organization’s latest reporting cycle is used. A listing names a probable source category for a water; it does not identify any particular farm, and it is not proof that a nearby operation on this map caused the impairment.

  • Feeding operations (3,262 assessment units): Animal Feeding Operations (NPS); Confined Animal Feeding Operations – CAFOs (Point Source); Confined Animal Feeding Operations (NPS); Livestock (Grazing or Feeding Operations); Manure Runoff; Animal Holding/Management Areas.
  • Grazing (4,499 assessment units): Grazing in Riparian or Shoreline Zones; Rangeland Grazing; Unrestricted Cattle Access; Managed Pasture Grazing.
  • Not included: Seafood Processing Operations; Animal Shows and Racetracks.

7,116 assessment units in total (one can be in both groups); for 3,121 the reporting organization marked the source as confirmed. 6,870 are mapped and 246 are list-only because the EPA ATTAINS geospatial service has no geometry for them. Rivers and lakes are long lines and areas, so each is shown as one representative point placed on the official ATTAINS geometry: a published point as is, the point halfway along a line, or a point inside an area. Follow the How’s My Waterway link for the full official shape. North Carolina, Minnesota, Nebraska, Kansas, Arkansas, Georgia and Missouri have no assessment units in their latest ATTAINS cycle that meet these criteria, so they have no points in this sub-layer.

USDA APHIS disease detections

These are confirmed detections from official USDA APHIS dashboard exports, downloaded 2026-11-25. APHIS publishes poultry detections at the county and state, and livestock / dairy detections at the state (plus a special ID). No official farm coordinates are in those exports, so nothing here is plotted as a point. A county or state with a detection is not the same as a mapped facility, and a detection is not by itself a water or soil contamination record.

How this was checked

  • National points and permits come from EPA’s ICIS-NPDES public download (npdes_downloads.zip, last-modified 2026-11-23 04:07 GMT, which is 2026-11-22 9:07 PM MST). CAFO permits are those with permit-component code CAF (6,734 IDs in the file). Cattle, poultry and hog SIC codes used: 0211, 0213, 0241, 0251, 0252, 0253, 0254, 0259, plus matching NAICS (112112, 112120, 112210, 1123xx).
  • Documented enforcement means a formal enforcement action and/or a single-event violation in that same ICIS-NPDES download. Informal enforcement and quarterly noncompliance history are stored on the record but do not by themselves put a site in the enforcement layer.
  • Iowa points come from the Iowa DNR Animal Feeding Operations Facilities map service (layer 3), queried 2026-11-25. Official latitude and longitude fields were used. Collection methods such as photo interpolation, public-land survey, GPS, or address matching are stored; address-matched points are marked approximate. Sites whose only animals are outside cattle, poultry and swine are omitted.
  • Coordinates are used only when those official files publish them. This project did not geocode addresses or place names, and it did not flip the sign on longitudes that appear to be missing a minus sign.
  • Every mapped point was tested against GADM 4.1 United States level-1 boundaries, with a 1 km coastal tolerance, matching the site’s existing U.S. location check. 296 national and Iowa points failed (241 of them with a positive, eastern-hemisphere longitude in the ICIS file, plus state-mismatch and offshore cases) and were withheld from the map. State agency points and ATTAINS representative points went through the same check; none failed.
  • EPA TRI industry-sector coverage does not include livestock and poultry production, so no TRI facilities were added.
  • Impaired waters come from the EPA ATTAINS Expert Query national sources file (refreshed 2026-11-20, 6:33 PM MST) and the EPA ATTAINS assessment geospatial service, queried 2026-11-26. No impaired water is linked to a specific operation on this map.
  • Verification labels: every stored field on these records was taken from the official file named in the source link (verified). No values were corrected or left unverified.
