Records request kit

A fill-in letter you can copy or print and send to a federal FOIA office or a state public-records office. Nothing you type here is sent or stored. This letter is not legal advice, and it does not file a request for you.

FOIA (5 U.S.C. 552) applies to federal agencies, not to private companies. For a contractor-operated plant, ask the federal or state agency that holds the permit or contract file. State and territorial public-records laws cover state and local agencies. Check the official page for the current address, portal, and any required form before you send the letter.
