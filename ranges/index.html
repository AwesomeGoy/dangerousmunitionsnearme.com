Firing and bombing ranges

Military firing, bombing and weapons-test ranges with documented contamination, unexploded ordnance or civilian incidents. 16 records: 14 verified, 0 corrected against a better official source and 2 unverified and marked as such.

This page is not an official government document and does not say any place is safe or unsafe. It is a selection of documented places, not a complete list. ZIP alerts on this site do not include these records.

Read before using this page

  • 2 records are unverified and labeled in yellow: Fallon Range Complex (NAS Fallon), Delamere Air Weapons Range. Figures in them are reported, not confirmed in an official source.
  • Areas are quoted two ways: the area printed by the operator, and the area computed from the DoD MIRTA boundary for U.S. ranges. They can differ.
  • Civilian incidents are reported as the official source describes them. No link between a range and a health outcome is claimed unless an official source makes it.
  • Blank coordinates mean list only. Nothing was geocoded or guessed.

7 records are mapped as points. 7 outlines are drawn (7 records). Unverified records have a dashed yellow outline. The other 9 records have no usable official coordinate and are listed below only.

  • Makua Military Reservation (Makua Valley), O'ahu — Hawaii, USA Verified
    Operator: U.S. Army (USAG Hawaii)

  • Kaho'olawe (former Navy bombing range) — Hawaii, USA Verified
    Operator: U.S. Navy (former); now Kaho'olawe Island Reserve Commission (State of Hawai'i)
    Status: former
    Years: 1941-1990 (bombing)
    Area (official): 28,800 acres (KIRC FY25)
    Hazards and contamination: UXO remains. Navy UXO Clearance Project ended in 2004: about 75% of the island surface-cleared, 10% (2,647 acres) also cleared to 4 ft, and 25% (6,692 acres) not cleared and unsafe for unescorted access (KIRC history). Surrounding waters not cleared (KIRC FY24/25).
    Cleanup and regulation: 1993 Title X, FY1994 DoD Appropriations Act: conveyance to the State and $400 million for ordnance removal. Deed conveyed 1994; access control returned 11 Nov 2003.
    List only: no official coordinate was found, so it is not on the map (not geocoded). Checked 2026-11-25.

  • Koon-ni (Kooni) air-to-ground range, Maehyang-ri, Hwaseong (former) — Gyeonggi-do, KOR Verified
    Operator: U.S. Air Force (former); site now Hwaseong city parks
    Status: former
    Years: 1951-2005 (closed Aug 2005)
    Hazards and contamination: US-forces-only bombing range centred on Nong-seom island, 1.6 km off Maehyang-ri. Polluted tidal flats and unprocessed bomb debris remained after closure (Gyeonggi).

    Cleanup and regulation: Site redeveloped as Maehyang-ri Peace Ecology Park (opened Sep 2021) and Kooni Memorial Garden; buildings registered in 2016 as Gyeonggi's first 'excellent architectural asset'.
