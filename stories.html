Neighbor stories

A place for first-person accounts from people who live, work, or went to school near a site in this tracker. A person reads every submission before anything is posted. Nothing is published automatically.

If you are 18 or older, you can send a short account of what you have seen or lived with near a named site. You may use a first name, initials, or “name withheld.” Your contact email is used only to follow up, and is not published. Home addresses and other sensitive personal data will be removed or will cause the story to be declined.

Read the moderation policy and the privacy notice before you submit.
