Neighbor stories

A planned place for first-person accounts from people who live, work, or went to school near a site in this tracker. Nothing is being collected yet. No story will be published automatically.

Submissions are not open yet. The form below is a preview only. It is disabled, it does not send or store anything, and there is no backend behind it. A moderation policy and a privacy notice have been drafted for D.A. Williams to review before any signup or story collection begins.

What this would be

If the feature is later approved, an adult could send a short account of what they have seen or lived with near a named site. A person would read every submission before anything is posted. Display names could be a first name, initials, or "name withheld." Contact email would be used only to follow up, not published. Home addresses and other sensitive personal data would be removed or would cause the story to be declined.

Until that review is finished, please do not email a story to the site author for publication through this page. The draft policy is not a request for submissions.
