What is documented

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of 12/01/2020. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This site did not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed or whether a new permit has been issued, unless a source below says so.
