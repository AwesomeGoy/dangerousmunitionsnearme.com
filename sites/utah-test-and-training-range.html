EPA FRS RCRAINFO UT0570090001 (NAD83).

Known energetics or munitions types

Utah RCRA permit Module III authorizes thermal treatment of hazardous-waste military munitions at UTTR in Box Elder County. Permitted wastes include unserviceable or serviceable excess munitions and explosive materials (examples in the module include bulk explosives, small-arms munitions, projectiles, flares, grenades, and sub-munitions) and unserviceable or serviceable excess solid-propellant components. The permittee may treat only hazardous-waste military munitions from allowed sources and is prohibited from treating hazardous waste from sources not authorized in the module. Treatment is described at Site 1 for open burning and Site 2 for open burning or open detonation. This draft does not have a current item-level inventory.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of August 18, 2025. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This draft did not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed or whether a new permit has been issued, unless a source below says so. The EPA-hosted file retrieved in this pass is the energetic-waste module, not a cover page naming the permittee; the EPA list name does not state a service branch, so the branch field is left blank.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this draft. That is not a finding that none occurred.

Notes

Utah DEQ maintains a UTTR facility-overview page that could not be retrieved in this pass (the request was blocked). Public descriptions that Hill Air Force Base operates UTTR were therefore not independently confirmed from a retrieved official page and are not used to fill the branch field.
