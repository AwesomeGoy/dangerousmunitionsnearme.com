EPA FRS RCRAINFO CA9570025149 (NAD83).

Known energetics or munitions types

The DTSC permit names the Permittee as the United States Air Force / Vandenberg Air Force Base (the permit text defines 'Air Force' as the United States Air Force and defines PEP materials as propellants, explosives, and pyrotechnic materials). The permitted Explosive Ordnance Disposal Range is used to treat military munitions and other PEP materials. This draft does not have a current item-level inventory. The permit title on the EPA list remains 'DEPT OF AIR FORCE VANDENBERG AFB'; this draft does not re-label the installation as Space Force.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of August 7, 2027. The permit recites earlier DTSC authorizations, including a 2008 EOD Range renewal that expired May 5, 2018, and a renewal application submitted March 2, 2018. This draft did not extract a single signature-block effective date for the current term beyond the EPA-listed expiration of August 7, 2027.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this draft. That is not a finding that none occurred.

Notes

Basic entry from the EPA OB/OD list and FRS coordinates only. Incidents and energetics inventory need research.
