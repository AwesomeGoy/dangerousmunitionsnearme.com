Known energetics or munitions types

M6 propellant and clean-burning igniter (CBI) left from Explo Systems contractor storage of Army-origin material. EPA approved contained-burn destruction of about 16 million pounds of M6 and CBI in June 2016. These are HD 1.3 propellant/igniter materials, not primary explosives such as lead azide.

Current status

The 2012–2016 emergency disposal campaign used a contained-burn chamber after open burning was dropped following public opposition. This draft has not independently confirmed current residual holdings, if any, after that campaign.

2016-06-13EPA approved a contained-burn system after tests showed 99.99% destruction of chemicals of concern, for about 16 million pounds of M6 and CBI.https://www.epa.gov/archive/epa/newsreleases/epa-approves-contained-burn-system-camp-minden-disposal.html
2016-09-29About 120,960 pounds of CBI auto-ignited in an earth-covered magazine and destroyed the bunker. Louisiana National Guard reported no injuries. An Army Explosive Safety Technical Assistance Visit then advised that remaining CBI was too dangerous to handle and should be destroyed in place, with some magazines remotely ignited because entering them was not acceptable.https://geauxguard.la.gov/2016/09/29/camp-minden-update-clean-burning-ignitor-combusted/
