Known energetics or munitions types

Utah RCRA permit Module V authorizes treatment of waste energetic material at the Dugway Thermal Treatment Facility, a vegetation-free area for open burning or open detonation equipped with three burn pans for open burning. The permittee is prohibited from treating at the DTTF, among other things, armor-penetrating weapons containing depleted uranium, and chemical and nuclear weapons, their devices, and components. The permit also states that chemical, radiological, and biological munitions require the Director's approval before emergency destruction when they have been determined unsafe to transport to the DTTF. This site does not have a current item-level inventory of conventional energetics treated at the DTTF.
