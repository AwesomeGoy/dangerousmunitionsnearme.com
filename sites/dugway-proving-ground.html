Known energetics or munitions types

Utah RCRA permit Module V authorizes treatment of waste energetic material at the Dugway Thermal Treatment Facility, a vegetation-free area for open burning or open detonation equipped with three burn pans for open burning. The permittee is prohibited from treating at the DTTF, among other things, armor-penetrating weapons containing depleted uranium, and chemical and nuclear weapons, their devices, and components. The permit also states that chemical, radiological, and biological munitions require the Director's approval before emergency destruction when they have been determined unsafe to transport to the DTTF. This draft does not have a current item-level inventory of conventional energetics treated at the DTTF.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of September 30, 2030, which had not yet been reached as of this check (Nov 23, 2026).

No notable incidents are recorded in this draft. That is not a finding that none occurred.
