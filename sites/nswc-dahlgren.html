Known energetics or munitions types

Virginia DEQ hazardous-waste permit issued to the United States Navy authorizes treatment of hazardous waste by open burning and open detonation at the Naval Surface Warfare Center Dahlgren Site (Churchill Range plans are among the permit attachments). This site does not record a complete item-level list from the permit attachments. The facility is a Navy weapons-development and test activity; a current inventory of energetics in storage was not retrieved.

Current status

The Virginia DEQ permit is effective as of April 28, 2021, and remains in effect until April 28, 2031, unless revoked, reissued, or terminated. EPA's current OB/OD list shows the facility as permitted with that April 28, 2031 expiration.
