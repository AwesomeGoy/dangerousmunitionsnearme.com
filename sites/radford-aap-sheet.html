What is documented

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of Sept 17, 2031. It is a production plant, not a JMC conventional-demil hub in the Oct 1, 2025 briefing.

Known energetics or munitions types

A production plant for energetic materials, including nitrocellulose (the material named in coverage of the June 2018 flash fire). This draft does not have a full current product inventory.

Prevailing wind

A 2016-2025 wind rose from the nearest Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS/AWOS station, DUBLIN/NEW RIV VLLY (PSK), 6.2 miles away. The most common wind is from the W (4.6% of non-calm observations in that sector). The rust arrow points downwind of that pattern. Calm was recorded 32.6% of the time.

This is a multi-year pattern of wind observed at the nearest airport or weather station, not a smoke or burn-day forecast. Wind at the munitions site can differ from the station. Direction is the direction the wind blows FROM, in the meteorological convention. Period: 2016-01-01 to 2025-12-31. Source: IEM wind rose for this station.
