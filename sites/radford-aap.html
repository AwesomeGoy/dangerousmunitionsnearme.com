Known energetics or munitions types

A production plant for energetic materials, including nitrocellulose (the material named in coverage of the June 2018 flash fire). This draft does not have a full current product inventory.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of Sept 17, 2031. It is a production plant, not a JMC conventional-demil hub in the Oct 1, 2025 briefing.
