EPA FRS RCRAINFO AZ5213820991 (NAD83). ADDRESS MATCHING-HOUSE NUMBER, accuracy_value=50, reference: ENTRANCE POINT OF A FACILITY OR STATION

Known energetics or munitions types

ADEQ permit for the Munitions Treatment Facility at U.S. Army Garrison Yuma Proving Ground authorizes open burning and open detonation of unserviceable, outdated, or obsolete munitions generated at the proving ground, in OB pads and OD pits. The explosive ordnance is categorized as reactive and ignitable hazardous waste. This draft does not have a current item-level inventory.

Current status

The ADEQ permit is effective as of July 11, 2017, and remains in effect for 10 years from that date unless continued under 40 CFR 270.51. EPA's current OB/OD list shows the facility as permitted with an expiration of July 11, 2027, which had not yet been reached as of this check (Nov 23, 2026). The permit requires a complete renewal application at least 180 days before expiration.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this draft. That is not a finding that none occurred.
