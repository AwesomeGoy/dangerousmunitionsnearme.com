Known energetics or munitions types

Nevada/EPA permit materials for the Main Base 'Old Bomb' unit describe open burning of waste propellants that cannot be reused, recycled, or reutilized, when closed treatment at the Western Area Demilitarization Facility is not available. Permitted waste codes cited in those materials include D003, D005, D008, D009, and D030. This draft does not have a current item-level inventory of the Hawthorne demil stockpile.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of May 21, 2025. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This draft does not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed. Hrycak's Oct 1, 2025 public-release demil briefing names Hawthorne Army Depot as a CONUS demil facility (GOCO).

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this draft. That is not a finding that none occurred.

Notes

EPA lists a second Hawthorne unit, New Bomb Facility (NV5210090010), as a separate OB/OD entry. FRS reports the same latitude/longitude for both IDs, with a stated accuracy of 5,000 meters; this draft does not invent a second point.
