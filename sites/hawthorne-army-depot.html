Known energetics or munitions types

Nevada/EPA permit materials for the Main Base 'Old Bomb' unit describe open burning of waste propellants that cannot be reused, recycled, or reutilized, when closed treatment at the Western Area Demilitarization Facility is not available. Permitted waste codes cited in those materials include D003, D005, D008, D009, and D030. This site does not have a current item-level inventory of the Hawthorne demil stockpile.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of May 21, 2025. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This site does not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed. Hrycak's Oct 1, 2025 public-release demil briefing names Hawthorne Army Depot as a CONUS demil facility (GOCO).

Hawthorne Main Base and the New Bomb Facility share one EPA FRS registry (110042074545) and one official map point, so they appear at the same spot on the map and the air and compliance data below are the same depot-wide records. See the New Bomb Facility.
