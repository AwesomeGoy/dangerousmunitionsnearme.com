Known energetics or munitions types

Hrycak's Oct 1, 2025 briefing names Crane Army Ammunition Activity as a CONUS demil facility at Crane, Indiana. EPA lists the RCRA OB/OD facility under Naval Support Activity Crane Division. This draft does not have a current item-level inventory.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of July 10, 2024. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This draft does not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed. Named as a JMC/CONUS demil hub in the Oct 1, 2025 public-release briefing.
