What is documented

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of September 6, 2033, which had not yet been reached as of this check (Nov 23, 2026).

Known energetics or munitions types

EPA lists Joint Base Charleston Weapons as a permitted OB/OD facility (EPA ID SC8170022620). The EPA-hosted hazardous-waste permit package was retrieved; this site records the EPA-listed expiration of September 6, 2033, and does not add an item-level munitions inventory that was not clearly extracted from the permit front matter in this pass.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this inventory. That is not a finding that none occurred.

Prevailing wind

A 2016-2025 wind rose from the nearest Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS/AWOS station, Charleston (CHS), 7.3 miles away. The most common wind is from the SSW (5.6% of non-calm observations in that sector). The rust arrow points downwind of that pattern. Calm was recorded 11.6% of the time.

This is a multi-year pattern of wind observed at the nearest airport or weather station, not a smoke or burn-day forecast. Wind at the munitions site can differ from the station. Direction is the direction the wind blows FROM, in the meteorological convention. Period: 2016-01-01 to 2025-12-31. Source: IEM wind rose for this station.
