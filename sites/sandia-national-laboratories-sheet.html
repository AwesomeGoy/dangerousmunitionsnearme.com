What is documented

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of 1/27/2025. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This site did not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed or whether a new permit has been issued, unless a source below says so.

Prevailing wind

A 2016-2025 wind rose from the nearest Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS/AWOS station, ALBUQUERQUE INTL (ABQ), 4.8 miles away. The most common wind is from the N (3.8% of non-calm observations in that sector). The rust arrow points downwind of that pattern. Calm was recorded 13.2% of the time.

This is a multi-year pattern of wind observed at the nearest airport or weather station, not a smoke or burn-day forecast. Wind at the munitions site can differ from the station.
