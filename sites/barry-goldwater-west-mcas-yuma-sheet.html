What is documented

Property owner and facility operator are the U.S. Marine Corps (MCAS Yuma). The ADEQ permit states it is effective as of August 4, 2015, and remains in effect for 10 years from that date, unless continued under 40 CFR 270.51; the signature block is dated July 16, 2021. EPA's current OB/OD list, last updated March 11, 2025, still shows the facility as permitted with an expiration of August 4, 2025. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This site did not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed or whether a new permit has been issued, unless a source below says so.

Known energetics or munitions types

ADEQ Arizona Hazardous Waste Management Act permit for the Munitions Treatment Range at Barry M. Goldwater Range–West: open detonation of unserviceable, outdated, or obsolete munitions generated at MCAS Yuma, other military bases and bombing ranges, and certain local government or contractor manufacturing facilities. Treatment is conducted in fragmentation-area and white-phosphorus-area detonation pits. The explosive ordnance is categorized as reactive and ignitable hazardous waste. This site does not have a current item-level inventory.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this inventory. That is not a finding that none occurred.
