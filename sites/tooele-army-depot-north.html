Known energetics or munitions types

Hrycak's Oct 1, 2025 briefing names Tooele Army Depot as a CONUS demil facility (GOGO). This site does not have a current item-level inventory.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of Sept 27, 2023. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This site does not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed.
