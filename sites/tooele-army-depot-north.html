Prevailing wind

A 2016-2025 wind rose from the nearest Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS/AWOS station, Tooele (TVY), 5.1 miles away. The most common wind is from the S (9.8% of non-calm observations in that sector). The rust arrow points downwind of that pattern. Calm was recorded 13.7% of the time.

This is a multi-year pattern of wind observed at the nearest airport or weather station, not a smoke or burn-day forecast. Wind at the munitions site can differ from the station. Direction is the direction the wind blows FROM, in the meteorological convention. Period: 2016-01-01 to 2025-12-31. Source: IEM wind rose for this station.

Known energetics or munitions types

Hrycak's Oct 1, 2025 briefing names Tooele Army Depot as a CONUS demil facility (GOGO). This draft does not have a current item-level inventory.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of Sept 27, 2023. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This draft does not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this draft. That is not a finding that none occurred.

Notes

EPA lists Tooele South (UT5210090002) separately. No comparable recent emergency lead-azide or spontaneous magazine-storage explosion was found in the Nov 23, 2026 research pass.
