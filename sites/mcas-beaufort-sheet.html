What is documented

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of January 29, 2025. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This site did not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed or whether a new permit has been issued, unless a source below says so.

Known energetics or munitions types

EPA lists MCAS Beaufort as a permitted OB/OD facility (EPA ID SC1750216169). An EPA-hosted Class 1 RCRA permit-modification package was retrieved. This site does not record an item-level inventory.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this inventory. That is not a finding that none occurred.

Prevailing wind

A 2016-2025 wind rose from the nearest Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS/AWOS station, BEAUFORT MCAS (NBC), 1.5 miles away. The most common wind is from the NNE (3.5% of non-calm observations in that sector). The rust arrow points downwind of that pattern. Calm was recorded 16.9% of the time.

This is a multi-year pattern of wind observed at the nearest airport or weather station, not a smoke or burn-day forecast. Wind at the munitions site can differ from the station. Direction is the direction the wind blows FROM, in the meteorological convention. Period: 2016-01-01 to 2025-12-31. Source: IEM wind rose for this station.
