Prevailing wind

A 2016-2025 wind rose from the nearest Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS/AWOS station, Richmond (RGA), 5.4 miles away. The most common wind is from the SSW (5.7% of non-calm observations in that sector). The rust arrow points downwind of that pattern. Calm was recorded 24.5% of the time.

This is a multi-year pattern of wind observed at the nearest airport or weather station, not a smoke or burn-day forecast. Wind at the munitions site can differ from the station. Known energetics or munitions types

Named as a CONUS conventional demil facility in the Oct 1, 2025 public-release briefing. Separately, Blue Grass was one of the last two U.S. chemical-weapons destruction sites. DoD announced that the final munition in the declared U.S. chemical-weapons stockpile — a sarin-filled M55 rocket — was destroyed there on July 7, 2023. This draft does not have a current item-level inventory of remaining conventional demil stocks.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of April 18, 2026. The declared U.S. chemical-weapons stockpile destruction operations were completed on July 7, 2023, at this depot. DoD said the chemical demil facilities would then enter a closure phase.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this draft. That is not a finding that none occurred.

Notes
