EPA FRS RCRAINFO NV5210090010 (NAD83). INTERPOLATION-PHOTO, accuracy_value=5000, reference: CENTER OF A FACILITY OR STATION

Known energetics or munitions types

EPA permit materials describe the New Bomb unit as an open-detonation unit of twenty earthen bermed cells used to deactivate explosive munitions that are unserviceable, unstable, or have no market for reuse or recycling, when processing at the Western Area Demilitarization Facility is not available.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of June 12, 2024. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This draft does not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this draft. That is not a finding that none occurred.

Notes

Same depot as Hawthorne Main Base (NV1210090006). FRS coordinates for the two IDs are identical, with 5,000-meter accuracy.
