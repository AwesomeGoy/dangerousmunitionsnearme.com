What is documented

The Georgia EPD permit was issued September 28, 2012, with a permit termination date of September 28, 2022 (application dated July 23, 2012). EPA's current OB/OD list, last updated March 11, 2025, still shows the facility as permitted with that September 28, 2022 expiration. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This site did not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed or whether a new permit has been issued, unless a source below says so.

Known energetics or munitions types

Georgia EPD Hazardous Waste Facility Permit HW-014(S&T)-4 (Facility I.D. GA4170090001) authorizes storage of 59,840 gallons of hazardous waste in containers; treatment of 0.5 short tons net explosive weight per day of waste munitions and explosives by open burning; and treatment of 0.6 short tons net explosive weight per day of waste munitions and explosives by open detonation; plus corrective action, including at the Old Camden County Landfill (Site 11). This site does not have a current item-level inventory.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this inventory. That is not a finding that none occurred.

Prevailing wind

A 2016-2025 wind rose from the nearest Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS/AWOS station, Fernandina Beach (FHB), 13.1 miles away. The most common wind is from the SSE (3.4% of non-calm observations in that sector). The rust arrow points downwind of that pattern. Calm was recorded 12.6% of the time.

This is a multi-year pattern of wind observed at the nearest airport or weather station, not a smoke or burn-day forecast. Wind at the munitions site can differ from the station.
