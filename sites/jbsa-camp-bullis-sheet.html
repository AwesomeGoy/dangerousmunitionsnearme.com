What is documented

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of May 23, 2029, which had not yet been reached as of this check (Nov 23, 2026).

Known energetics or munitions types

EPA lists Joint Base San Antonio–Camp Bullis as a permitted OB/OD facility (EPA ID TX4210020133). An EPA-hosted Texas industrial solid-waste / hazardous-waste permit package was retrieved. This site does not record an item-level inventory from the permit attachments.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this inventory. That is not a finding that none occurred.

Prevailing wind

A 2016-2025 wind rose from the nearest Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS/AWOS station, San Antonio (5C1), 8.3 miles away. The most common wind is from the S (8.1% of non-calm observations in that sector). The rust arrow points downwind of that pattern. Calm was recorded 29.3% of the time.

This is a multi-year pattern of wind observed at the nearest airport or weather station, not a smoke or burn-day forecast. Wind at the munitions site can differ from the station. Direction is the direction the wind blows FROM, in the meteorological convention. Period: 2016-01-01 to 2025-12-31. Source: IEM wind rose for this station.
