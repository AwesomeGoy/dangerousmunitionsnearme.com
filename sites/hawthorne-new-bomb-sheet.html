What is documented

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of June 12, 2024. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This site does not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed.

Known energetics or munitions types

EPA permit materials describe the New Bomb unit as an open-detonation unit of twenty earthen bermed cells used to deactivate explosive munitions that are unserviceable, unstable, or have no market for reuse or recycling, when processing at the Western Area Demilitarization Facility is not available.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this inventory. That is not a finding that none occurred.

A 2016-2025 wind rose from the nearest Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS/AWOS station, Hawthorne (HTH), 1.3 miles away. The most common wind is from the NW (8.7% of non-calm observations in that sector). The rust arrow points downwind of that pattern. Calm was recorded 19.9% of the time.
