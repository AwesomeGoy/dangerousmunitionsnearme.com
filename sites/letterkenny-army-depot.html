Known energetics or munitions types

Hrycak's Oct 1, 2025 briefing names Letterkenny Munitions Center (LEMC) at Chambersburg, Pennsylvania, as a CONUS demil facility (GOGO), and mentions an ammonium perchlorate rocket-motor destruction capability there. This draft does not have a current item-level inventory.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of Aug 5, 2018. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This draft does not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this draft. That is not a finding that none occurred.

Basic entry from the EPA OB/OD list and FRS coordinates only. Incidents and energetics inventory need research.
