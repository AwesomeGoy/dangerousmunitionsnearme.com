Known energetics or munitions types

A small-caliber ammunition production plant. The April 11, 2017 fatal accident occurred in primer-mixing operations. Primers commonly use primary explosives; this draft does not treat a specific chemical composition as confirmed from a primary document in this pass.

Current status

An active Army ammunition plant as of the 2017 accident reporting. It is not on EPA's current OB/OD operating list. Current production status was not independently re-verified beyond that historical coverage.
