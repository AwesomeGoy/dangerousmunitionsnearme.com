Known energetics or munitions types

Part of Naval Surface Warfare Center Indian Head Division. The EPA facility document posted June 2025 states that RCRA Part B, Subpart X permit applications for thermal treatment of explosives were submitted to EPA on November 8, 1988, for both the Main Area and the Stump Neck Annex, and that both remain in interim status. A historical Part B application describes Stump Neck as the smaller peninsula and notes a tenant Naval Explosive Ordnance Disposal Technology Center operation there. This draft does not have a current item-level inventory.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as interim status, with permit expiration 'Not applicable.' A final miscellaneous-unit permit for the annex was not identified as having been issued in the sources reviewed.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this draft. That is not a finding that none occurred.
