Known energetics or munitions types

Bulk lead azide stored wetted in 68 55-gallon plastic drums in two earth-covered magazines; Col. Aaron C. Teller (71st Ordnance Group EOD) states the total weight is 9,773.5 lb. The plant also has a conventional munitions load-assemble-pack mission. This site does not inventory other energetics on site.

Current status

As of Nov 23, 2026 (America/Phoenix): the planned in-place detonation had not occurred. The original date was Nov 20; the Army postponed to Nov 23, then announced Nov 25. The Eighth Circuit issued an administrative stay of EPA's emergency treatment permit on Nov 19, pending a ruling on the emergency stay motion in No. 26-3374; as of a CourtListener docket review on Nov 23, that merits ruling had not issued. Iowa Public Radio reported that President Trump posted on Truth Social on Nov 23 that he ordered the military not to proceed until the Eighth Circuit agrees they can go forward, and subject to Gov.-elect Rob Sand agreeing. IAAAP also holds a regular RCRA OB/OD permit (EPA ID IA7213820445) listed as permitted through Sept 28, 2028. The emergency permit for in-place magazine detonation is a separate authorization, issued Nov 13, 2026, effective through Feb 11, 2027.

Notes

The closest documented historical analog for bulk lead azide in deteriorating 55-gallon drums is Savanna Army Depot (1984). A March 21, 2014 Army article attributed DoD's 2004 green-primaries work to concern over a 40-year-old deteriorating lead azide stockpile at IAAAP. A separate research log kept with this site has the claim-by-claim review.
