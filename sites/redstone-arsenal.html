Known energetics or munitions types

ADEM's June 2026 draft-modification fact sheet says Redstone is a U.S. Army Aviation and Missile Command installation. The OB/OD area is used to burn and detonate hazardous energetic waste with the characteristic of reactivity (D003) and explosiveness. The open-burning area consists of five burn pans on concrete pads; open detonation takes place in a designated OD area. Explosive Destruction System (EDS) units are used to treat recovered munitions or other items. This draft does not have a current item-level inventory.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of July 18, 2031. ADEM issued a 10-year hazardous-waste permit renewal effective July 19, 2021 (EPA ID AL7210020742 / AL 7 210 020 742). ADEM published a draft permit modification fact sheet for public comment June 3–July 20, 2026, to incorporate investigations and corrective measures approved since the last modification (August 8, 2022). This draft did not independently confirm whether that 2026 modification was finalized.
