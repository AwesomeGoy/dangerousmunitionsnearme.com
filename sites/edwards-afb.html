Known energetics or munitions types

California DTSC RCRA-equivalent permit: the Explosive Ordnance Disposal Range in the Precision Impact Range Area has open-burn and open-detonation units. Edwards AFB generates waste munitions, energetic rocket propellant, and propellant-contaminated laboratory materials. The permit describes OB as self-sustained combustion used primarily to treat rocket propellant, and OD as detonation initiated by a donor charge, used primarily to treat waste munitions. This site does not have a current item-level inventory.

Current status

Permittee is the United States Air Force. DTSC permit first-issue date September 20, 2021; effective October 20, 2021; expiration September 20, 2031. EPA's current OB/OD list matches that expiration date and shows the facility as permitted.
