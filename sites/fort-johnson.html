Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as 'US ARMY, FORT JOHNSON,' permitted, with a listed expiration of October 31, 2020. EPA's RCRA/CIMC profile for the same EPA ID (LA0214022725) also appears under the earlier name Fort Polk. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This draft did not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed or whether a new permit has been issued, unless a source below says so. This pass did not retrieve a current LDEQ hazardous-waste operating permit text or a documented renewal decision for the OB/OD units.
