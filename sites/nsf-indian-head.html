Known energetics or munitions types

An EPA federal-facility document (posted June 2025) states that Naval Surface Warfare Center Indian Head Division — formerly the Naval Ordnance Station, Naval Propellant Plant, Naval Powder Factory, and Naval Proving Grounds — is the lead Navy activity for propellants, explosives, and pyrotechnics (energetics), and conducts engineering, manufacturing, research and development, and testing of energetic materials and ordnance devices. The same document says a RCRA Part B, Subpart X permit application for thermal treatment of explosives was submitted to EPA on November 8, 1988, for both the Main Area and the Stump Neck Annex, and that both activities remain in interim status for those miscellaneous units. The installation was added to the Superfund National Priorities List in September 1995. This site does not have a current item-level inventory.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as interim status, with permit expiration 'Not applicable.' Interim status has continued since the 1988 Part B Subpart X applications; a final miscellaneous-unit permit was not identified as having been issued in the sources reviewed.

No notable incidents are recorded in this inventory. That is not a finding that none occurred.
