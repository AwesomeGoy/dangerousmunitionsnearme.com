Known energetics or munitions types

Georgia EPD Hazardous Waste Facility Permit HW-014(S&T)-4 (Facility I.D. GA4170090001) authorizes storage of 59,840 gallons of hazardous waste in containers; treatment of 0.5 short tons net explosive weight per day of waste munitions and explosives by open burning; and treatment of 0.6 short tons net explosive weight per day of waste munitions and explosives by open detonation; plus corrective action, including at the Old Camden County Landfill (Site 11). This draft does not have a current item-level inventory.

Current status

The Georgia EPD permit was issued September 28, 2012, with a permit termination date of September 28, 2022 (application dated July 23, 2012). EPA's current OB/OD list, last updated March 11, 2025, still shows the facility as permitted with that September 28, 2022 expiration. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This draft did not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed or whether a new permit has been issued, unless a source below says so.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this draft. That is not a finding that none occurred.

Notes

The EPA-hosted permit PDF is a scan; the cover terms were read from the rendered first page. This pass did not retrieve a post-2022 renewal decision.
