What is documented

An active Army ammunition plant as of the 2017 accident reporting. It is not on EPA's current OB/OD operating list. Current production status was not independently re-verified beyond that historical coverage.

Known energetics or munitions types

A small-caliber ammunition production plant. The April 11, 2017 fatal accident occurred in primer-mixing operations. Primers commonly use primary explosives; this site does not treat a specific chemical composition as confirmed from a primary document in this pass.

Prevailing wind

A 2016-2025 wind rose from the nearest Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS/AWOS station, Lee s Summit (LXT), 5.2 miles away. The most common wind is from the S (5.7% of non-calm observations in that sector). The rust arrow points downwind of that pattern. Calm was recorded 5.4% of the time.

This is a multi-year pattern of wind observed at the nearest airport or weather station, not a smoke or burn-day forecast. Wind at the munitions site can differ from the station.
