What is documented

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of September 30, 2030, which had not yet been reached as of this check (Nov 23, 2026).

Known energetics or munitions types

Utah RCRA permit Module V authorizes treatment of waste energetic material at the Dugway Thermal Treatment Facility, a vegetation-free area for open burning or open detonation equipped with three burn pans for open burning. The permittee is prohibited from treating at the DTTF, among other things, armor-penetrating weapons containing depleted uranium, and chemical and nuclear weapons, their devices, and components. The permit also states that chemical, radiological, and biological munitions require the Director's approval before emergency destruction when they have been determined unsafe to transport to the DTTF. This site does not have a current item-level inventory of conventional energetics treated at the DTTF.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this inventory. That is not a finding that none occurred.

Prevailing wind

A 2016-2025 wind rose from the nearest Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS/AWOS station, DUGWAY PRVG GROUND (DPG), 9.9 miles away. The most common wind is from the WNW (5.4% of non-calm observations in that sector). The rust arrow points downwind of that pattern. Calm was recorded 10.1% of the time.

This is a multi-year pattern of wind observed at the nearest airport or weather station, not a smoke or burn-day forecast. Wind at the munitions site can differ from the station.
