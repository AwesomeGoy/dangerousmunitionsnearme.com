Prevailing wind

Known energetics or munitions types

EPA lists Joint Base Charleston Weapons as a permitted OB/OD facility (EPA ID SC8170022620). The EPA-hosted hazardous-waste permit package was retrieved; this site records the EPA-listed expiration of September 6, 2033, and does not add an item-level munitions inventory that was not clearly extracted from the permit front matter in this pass.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of September 6, 2033, which had not yet been reached as of this check (Nov 23, 2026).
