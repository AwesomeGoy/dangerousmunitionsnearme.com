Known energetics or munitions types

Florida DEP operating permit 70725-008-HO: low explosives are treated by burning in an Open Burn Unit consisting of two steel burn boxes; high explosives are detonated at an Open Detonation Unit used for fragment-producing items. A September 23, 2022 minor modification authorized in-kind replacement of the Open Burn Unit. This draft does not have a current item-level inventory.

Current status

Permit issued March 19, 2020, to Cape Canaveral Space Force Station / 45 CES/CD; the permit face states an expiration date of March 3, 2025. EPA's current OB/OD list, last updated March 11, 2025, still shows the facility as permitted with that expiration date. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This draft did not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed or whether a new permit has been issued, unless a source below says so.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this draft. That is not a finding that none occurred.
