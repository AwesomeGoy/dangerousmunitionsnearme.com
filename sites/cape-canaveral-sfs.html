Known energetics or munitions types

Florida DEP operating permit 70725-008-HO: low explosives are treated by burning in an Open Burn Unit consisting of two steel burn boxes; high explosives are detonated at an Open Detonation Unit used for fragment-producing items. A September 23, 2022 minor modification authorized in-kind replacement of the Open Burn Unit. This site does not have a current item-level inventory.

Current status

Permit issued March 19, 2020, to Cape Canaveral Space Force Station / 45 CES/CD; the permit face states an expiration date of March 3, 2025.
