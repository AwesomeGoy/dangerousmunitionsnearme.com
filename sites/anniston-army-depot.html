Known energetics or munitions types

Hrycak's Oct 1, 2025 briefing names Anniston Defense Munitions Center (ANMC) at Anniston, Alabama, as a CONUS demil facility (GOGO). Anniston was also a completed chemical-weapons destruction site in the earlier continental campaign that DoD said was finished by 2012. This draft does not have a current item-level inventory of conventional demil stocks.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of Sept 21, 2031.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this draft. That is not a finding that none occurred.

Basic entry from the EPA OB/OD list and FRS coordinates only. Incidents and energetics inventory need research.
