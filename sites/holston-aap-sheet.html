What is documented

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of March 31, 2021. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This site does not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed.

Known energetics or munitions types

A production plant historically associated with RDX and Composition B manufacturing. This site does not treat that product list as a current verified inventory.

Prevailing wind

A 2016-2025 wind rose from the nearest Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS/AWOS station, BRISTOL/TRI-CITY (TRI), 13.7 miles away. The most common wind is from the WSW (3.9% of non-calm observations in that sector). The rust arrow points downwind of that pattern. Calm was recorded 48.3% of the time.

This is a multi-year pattern of wind observed at the nearest airport or weather station, not a smoke or burn-day forecast. Wind at the munitions site can differ from the station. Direction is the direction the wind blows FROM, in the meteorological convention. Period: 2016-01-01 to 2025-12-31. Source: IEM wind rose for this station.
