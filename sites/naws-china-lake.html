Known energetics or munitions types

EPA and DTSC materials describe operations that include research and development of explosive materials and weapons. A DTSC fact sheet for the hazardous-waste permit describes on-site treatment of reactive energetics at Burro Canyon by open burn and open detonation, including obsolete or expired munitions and explosive laboratory/propellant wastes. The EPA-hosted permit PDF retrieved in this pass was image-based and could not be text-extracted here; annual and per-event limits reported in secondary descriptions were not independently read from the signed permit text in this draft, so those numbers are not recorded. EPA's current OB/OD list shows the permit as expiring July 12, 2028.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of July 12, 2028. EPA announced in 2022 that 2018 inspections had found RCRA violations (including deteriorating or open hazardous-waste containers and improper universal-waste management); EPA said the facility had resolved the violations and was then in compliance.
