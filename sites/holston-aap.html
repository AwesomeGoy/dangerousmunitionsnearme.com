Known energetics or munitions types

A production plant historically associated with RDX and Composition B manufacturing. This draft does not treat that product list as a current verified inventory.

Current status

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of March 31, 2021. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This draft does not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed.
