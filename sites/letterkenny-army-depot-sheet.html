What is documented

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of Aug 5, 2018. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This site does not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed.

Known energetics or munitions types

Hrycak's Oct 1, 2025 briefing names Letterkenny Munitions Center (LEMC) at Chambersburg, Pennsylvania, as a CONUS demil facility (GOGO), and mentions an ammonium perchlorate rocket-motor destruction capability there. This site does not have a current item-level inventory.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this inventory. That is not a finding that none occurred.

Prevailing wind

A 2016-2025 wind rose from the nearest Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS/AWOS station, HAGERSTOWN REGIONAL (HGR), 20.4 miles away. The most common wind is from the S (5.1% of non-calm observations in that sector). The rust arrow points downwind of that pattern. Calm was recorded 15.0% of the time.

This is a multi-year pattern of wind observed at the nearest airport or weather station, not a smoke or burn-day forecast. Wind at the munitions site can differ from the station.
