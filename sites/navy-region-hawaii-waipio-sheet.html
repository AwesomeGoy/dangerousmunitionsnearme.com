What is documented

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as permitted, with a listed expiration of January 29, 2029. Hawaii DOH solicited public comment beginning August 15, 2024, on a draft five-year RAP permit renewal for continued controlled intentional detonations at the Waipio Peninsula Annex. This site did not independently retrieve a final 2024–2025 RAP issuance. In 2015, DOH granted a 90-day State Emergency Hazardous Waste Permit on June 29, 2015, allowing up to 50 detonation events and no more than 15 pounds net explosive weight per event, to destroy munitions recovered from dredged material.

Known energetics or munitions types

The site is used to treat remediation-waste munitions recovered from dredging and construction-related activities at Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam. A July 11, 2015 Navy release said recovered types from Pearl Harbor dredged-material screening included 5-inch projectiles, 4.5-inch barrage rockets and/or motors, 3-inch and 75mm projectiles, 60mm mortars, 40mm and 20mm projectiles and/or casings, grenades, a practice bomb, a TNT demolition charge, fuzes, and small-arms ammunition. A Hawaii Department of Health public notice dated August 15, 2024, on a draft five-year Remedial Action Plan permit renewal, would limit each detonation shot to no more than 16 pounds net explosive weight unless DOH approves a larger shot, and would cap annual treatment at 5,760 pounds net explosive weight per 365-day period. Those numeric limits are from the 2024 draft public notice, not from a final permit page retrieved in this pass.
