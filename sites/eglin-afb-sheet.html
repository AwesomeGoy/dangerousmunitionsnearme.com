What is documented

The EPA-hosted permit face lists an expiration date of September 1, 2025. EPA's current OB/OD list, last updated March 11, 2025, shows the facility as permitted with that expiration. A public March 2025 renewal application exists for the open-detonation range. EPA notes that conditions of an expired permit continue in force if the permittee has submitted a timely application for a new permit (40 CFR 270.51). This site did not independently confirm whether a timely renewal was filed or whether a new permit has been issued, unless a source below says so.

Known energetics or munitions types

The EPA-hosted Florida DEP permit package identifies the facility as a permitted open-detonation operation (EPA ID FL8570024366). A March 2025 open-detonation permit renewal application for Range C-62 has been circulated in the public record; this site did not independently re-read that entire application for a waste-by-waste inventory. The current listed permit expiration is September 1, 2025.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this inventory. That is not a finding that none occurred.

Prevailing wind

A 2016-2025 wind rose from the nearest Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS/AWOS station, EGLIN AFB/VALPARAIS (VPS), 1.9 miles away. The most common wind is from the N (4.1% of non-calm observations in that sector). The rust arrow points downwind of that pattern. Calm was recorded 16.2% of the time.

This is a multi-year pattern of wind observed at the nearest airport or weather station, not a smoke or burn-day forecast. Wind at the munitions site can differ from the station. Direction is the direction the wind blows FROM, in the meteorological convention. Period: 2016-01-01 to 2025-12-31. Source: IEM wind rose for this station.
