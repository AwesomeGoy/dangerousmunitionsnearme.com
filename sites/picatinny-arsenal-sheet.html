What is documented

Listed on EPA's current OB/OD facilities table as interim status (permit expiration 'Not applicable').

Known energetics or munitions types

Picatinny has a long official role in energetics research, including work on alternatives to lead azide. A March 21, 2014 Army article said that work on 'green' primary explosives started in 2004 in response to DoD concerns over a 40-year-old stockpile of lead azide deteriorating at IAAAP. This draft does not have a current inventory of energetics stored at Picatinny.

Notable incidents

No notable incidents are recorded in this draft. That is not a finding that none occurred.
