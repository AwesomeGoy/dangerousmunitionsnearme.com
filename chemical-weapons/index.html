Chemical weapons and sea dumps

Chemical weapons stockpiles and their destruction, wartime and post-war sea dumps, and notable chemical weapons incidents, from OPCW, U.S. Army, HELCOM and national records. 63 records: 58 verified, 1 corrected against a better official source and 4 unverified and marked as such.

This page is not an official government document and does not say any place is safe or unsafe. It is a selection of documented places, not a complete list. ZIP alerts on this site do not include these records.

Read before using this page

  • 4 records are unverified and labeled in yellow: South-west of Bornholm (off Rønne) reported chemical munitions dumping (unverified), Gdańsk Deep chemical munition dumpsite (HELCOM polygon), Operation CHASE ship-sinking disposals (U.S., 1960s-1970) — unverified, Qiqihar abandoned chemical weapons mustard exposure at a construction site. Figures in them are reported, not confirmed in an official source.
  • Destruction dates and quantities are as the OPCW, U.S. Army or national government gives them. Many stockpile sites outside the U.S. have no official coordinate and are list only.
  • Dump-area outlines are official HELCOM polygons supplied to EMODnet. Several Baltic dumps are “reported” only; HELCOM itself calls some unverified, and they are labeled.
  • Blank coordinates mean list only. Nothing was geocoded or guessed.

14 records are mapped as points. 13 outlines are drawn (7 records). Unverified records have a dashed yellow outline. The other 44 records have no usable official coordinate and are listed below only.
