About language on this site

The menus, labels and disclaimers are shown in the language you choose. Record names, research notes and quotations stay in English. Source titles stay in their original language. They are not machine-translated.

Neighbor stories moderation policy

The Neighbor stories page lets people who live, work, or went to school near a tracked site describe what they have seen or lived with. Stories appear on dangerousmunitionsnearme.com only after a person reviews them. Nothing is published automatically.

What is accepted

  • First-person accounts about living, working, or going to school near a named site in the tracker.
  • Observations of burning, detonations, smoke, odors, noise, closures, or official notices, written as the submitter’s experience.
  • Short context (town, years, distance) that helps a reader understand the account.
  • Submissions from adults only (18 or older).

What is rejected or removed

  • Anything that identifies a minor, or that appears to be from a minor.
  • Home addresses, phone numbers, exact current locations of private homes, Social Security numbers, medical record numbers, or other sensitive personal data. If a useful story includes those, they are removed before publication or the story is declined.
  • Defamation, threats, or incitement; unsubstantiated accusations against named private individuals.
  • Commercial advertising, spam, or coordinated inauthentic posts.
  • Copyrighted material the submitter does not have the right to share.
  • Medical diagnoses or legal claims written as proven fact when they are not sourced. Health and legal claims are framed as the submitter’s account, not as findings of this site.
  • Content that is primarily political campaigning unrelated to a site.

Review steps

  1. Submissions arrive only through the form on the Neighbor stories page.
  2. A human reviewer (D.A. Williams or a person he designates) reads every submission before it is published. Nothing goes up automatically.
  3. The reviewer may edit for length, spelling, and to remove personal data, and may ask the submitter to confirm a fact or a redaction. Edits will not change the meaning of the account.
  4. Each published story is labeled as a reader submission, with the date received, the related site, and a note that it is not an official finding.
  5. The reviewer may decline a story without a detailed public explanation when publishing it would risk someone’s safety or privacy.

Attribution

Submitters may choose a first name and town, initials, or “name withheld.” Full legal names are not required and are not added by the site.

Corrections and takedowns

A submitter may request a correction or removal of their own story. A person who is clearly identified in a story may request a correction of a specific factual error. Requests are considered promptly; illegal content is removed. Write to D.A. Williams through awesomegoy.com.
