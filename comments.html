How to find RCRA permit notices

EPA describes formal opportunities to comment on hazardous-waste (RCRA) permits, including written comments and public hearings, in How Can the Public Participate in the Hazardous Waste Permitting Process? Those procedures are in 40 C.F.R. Parts 124 and 270. States that run the RCRA program may have additional rules.

  • Ask the EPA regional office or the state environmental agency to put you on the facility mailing list for notices and fact sheets.
  • Look up the facility in EPA’s Enforcement and Compliance History Online (ECHO) and follow links to the permit record.
  • Watch official public notices, including the Federal Register, Regulations.gov, and the state or EPA regional public-notice page for the site.
  • EPA says that, under the federal rules, a draft permit or a notice of intent to deny is announced with a public comment period (typically 45 days), and that a public hearing is held if someone asks for one in writing or if the agency decides to hold one.

This tracker only lists a period when a sourced record is in the optional comment-period file. An empty list is not a finding that no notices exist.
