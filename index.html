About this data

This site isn’t the only source. It gathers official public records into one searchable place: the U.S. EPA’s current open-burning and open-detonation facilities list, Canada’s Department of National Defence unexploded ordnance (UXO) legacy sites, EMODnet’s European dumped munitions data, EPA’s Superfund National Priorities List (data as of September 30, 2026), and the Department of Defense PFAS status chart (data as of June 30, 2026), plus the other sources listed on each page. FUSRAP nuclear cleanup sites come from the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, the DOE Office of Legacy Management and EPA location records, checked Nov 25, 2026. The Australia & New Zealand section uses the Australian Defence UXO map (updated Nov 16, 2026), Defence PFAS program reports (status Oct 2024 and Annual Report 2024-25), NZDF, Ministry for the Environment and NZ EPA PFAS records (2018 to 2026), LINZ sea-dumping notices (July 2025) and place names, and state and council contaminated-land registers (counts read Nov 25, 2026). U.S. sea-dump sites come from DoD’s FY2009 sea-disposal report, checked Nov 25, 2026; only Ordnance Reef off O‘ahu is mapped, from a NOAA chart. DOE Legacy Management sites come from DOE’s LM site list and GEMS map service (updated Nov 24, 2026), and Navajo-area abandoned uranium mines from EPA Region 9, both checked Nov 25, 2026. Always check the original agency source and follow its safety guidance.

A sourced tracker of facilities that store or destroy old or unstable energetics, starting from EPA's current open-burning / open-detonation list and a few documented comparables. 67 sites are in this cut, including Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, other DoD, Energy, NASA, and contractor facilities. It also includes 727 military installations the Department of Defense is investigating for PFAS (212 with checked map points, the rest listed by state), 1,840 EPA Superfund sites, 20 active FUSRAP nuclear cleanup sites, 72 U.S. ocean munitions dump sites, 108 DOE Legacy Management sites, 523 abandoned uranium mines on and near the Navajo Nation and an Australia & New Zealand section. Blank fields mean no source was found — nothing here is guessed.

Is there one near me?

Search one ZIP code to see the closest munitions sites, military PFAS sites, Superfund cleanup sites and FUSRAP atomic-era cleanup sites. New: What is PFAS? · What is Superfund? · What is FUSRAP?

Enter a five-digit U.S. ZIP code to see the closest sites in this inventory. The map will zoom to that ZIP.

Distances are straight-line miles from the ZIP Code Tabulation Area (ZCTA) center published in the U.S. Census Bureau 2025 gazetteer, or from a location this browser reports after you click “Use my location.” They are not driving distance and not a measure of risk. Only sites in this dataset are included. Not every postal ZIP has a ZCTA centroid. Your location is not sent to a server.
