About this data

This site isn’t the only source. It gathers official public records into one searchable place: the U.S. EPA’s current open-burning and open-detonation facilities list, Canada’s Department of National Defence unexploded ordnance (UXO) legacy sites, and EMODnet’s European dumped munitions data, plus the other sources listed on each page. Always check the original agency source and follow its safety guidance.

A sourced tracker of facilities that store or destroy old or unstable energetics, starting from EPA's current open-burning / open-detonation list and a few documented comparables. 67 sites are in this cut, including Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, other DoD, Energy, NASA, and contractor facilities. Blank fields mean no source was found — nothing here is guessed.

Is there one near me?

Enter a five-digit U.S. ZIP code to see the closest sites in this inventory. The map will zoom to that ZIP.

Distances are straight-line miles from the ZIP Code Tabulation Area (ZCTA) center published in the U.S. Census Bureau 2025 gazetteer, or from a location this browser reports after you click “Use my location.” They are not driving distance and not a measure of risk. Only sites in this dataset are included. Not every postal ZIP has a ZCTA centroid. Your location is not sent to a server.
