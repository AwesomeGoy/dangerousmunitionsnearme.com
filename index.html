A draft, sourced tracker of facilities that store or destroy old or unstable energetics, starting from EPA's current open-burning / open-detonation list and a few documented comparables. 67 sites are in this cut, including Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, other DoD, Energy, NASA, and contractor facilities. Blank fields mean no source was found — nothing here is guessed.

Is there one near me?

Enter a five-digit U.S. ZIP code to see the closest sites in this draft. The map will zoom to that ZIP.

Distances are straight-line miles from the ZIP Code Tabulation Area (ZCTA) center published in the U.S. Census Bureau 2025 gazetteer, or from a location this browser reports after you click “Use my location.” They are not driving distance and not a measure of risk. Only sites in this draft dataset are included. Not every postal ZIP has a ZCTA centroid. Your location is not sent to a server.
