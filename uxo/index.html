Wartime unexploded ordnance

Countries and areas still contaminated by landmines, cluster munitions and other unexploded wartime ordnance, with official figures where published. 14 records: 11 verified, 1 corrected against a better official source and 2 unverified and marked as such.

This page is not an official government document and does not say any place is safe or unsafe. It is a selection of documented places, not a complete list. ZIP alerts on this site do not include these records.

Read before using this page

  • 2 records are unverified and labeled in yellow: Azerbaijan: mine and ERW contamination of the Karabakh and East Zangezur regions, France: WWI/WWII battlefield munitions (incl. the former 'Zone Rouge' around Verdun). Figures in them are reported, not confirmed in an official source.
  • No national outlines are drawn. Contamination figures come from national mine-action authorities and treaty reports; the only outline is the SS Richard Montgomery exclusion zone defined by the UK Maritime and Coastguard Agency.
  • Context from mine-action organizations is labeled as not official.
  • Blank coordinates mean list only. Nothing was geocoded or guessed.

0 records are mapped as points. 1 outlines are drawn (1 records). Unverified records have a dashed yellow outline. The other 13 records have no usable official coordinate and are listed below only.

  • Germany: WWII bombs and munitions, Brandenburg incl. Oranienburg — Brandenburg, DEU Verified
    Contamination: WWII aircraft bombs (many with chemical long-delay fuzes), munitions
    Conflict: 1939-1945
    Official figures: Brandenburg KMBD 2025: ~360 t of munitions found; 897 ha released from suspicion by end November (MIK, 22 Dec 2025). Oranienburg: 233 high-explosive bombs found Oct 1991-Nov 2024, ~55% with dangerous fuzes (many chemical long-delay), 109 defused, 34 blown up in place (state government). MIK: no region in Germany is known where WWII consequences lie so heavily in the ground. (as of 2025)
    Clearance: Ongoing; Oranienburg model region since 2019.
    List only: no official coordinate was found, so it is not on the map (not geocoded). Checked 2026-11-25.

  • SS Richard Montgomery wreck with ~1,400 t of explosives, Thames Estuary — England, GBR Verified
    Contamination: WWII munitions cargo in a wreck
    Conflict: 1944 grounding
    Official figures: US Liberty ship that grounded on Sheerness Middle Sand on 20 Aug 1944 carrying 6,127 imperial tons of cargo, mainly munitions; 2,954 t were salvaged; approximately 1,400 t NEQ remain in forward holds 1-3; 1.5 miles from Sheerness, 5 miles from Southend; designated a dangerous wreck under s.2 Protection of Wrecks Act 1973 with a no-entry exclusion zone (MCA). 2024 survey: forward section still tilting east (43-55 cm shift at the foremast 2022-24). (as of 2024-2025 surveys)
    Clearance: Monitored by annual MCA surveys; not cleared.
    Shown on the map as the official exclusion zone; no point is drawn. Checked 2026-11-25.
    Sources: UK Maritime and Coastguard Agency (GOV.UK), 'SS Richard Montgomery: background information' · MCA, 'SS Richard Montgomery: Survey report 2024' (2 Dec 2024; read in a search summary) · MCA, 'SS Richard Montgomery: Survey report 2025'
  • Okinawa: Battle of Okinawa unexploded ordnance — Okinawa, JPN Verified
    Contamination: WWII UXO (shells, bombs)
    Conflict: 1945 Battle of Okinawa
    Official figures: About 200,000 t of ammunition was used in Okinawa; an estimated 5% (10,000 t) remained as UXO; ~3,000 t were cleared by residents and ~2,500 t by US forces before 1972, and ~2,122 t by the SDF to FY2022; allowing 500 t never to be found, ~1,878 t remained buried (FY2022 yearbook). FY1972-FY2024 total: 40,825 disposals, 2,156.2 t (FY2024 yearbook). A March 1974 UXO explosion during sewer work in Oroku, Naha, killed or injured 30-odd people. (as of FY2022 / FY2024 yearbooks)
    Clearance: Ongoing (GSDF 101st UXO Disposal Unit).
    List only: no official coordinate was found, so it is not on the map (not geocoded). Checked 2026-11-25.
    Sources: Okinawa Prefecture, 消防防災年報 (fire and disaster-prevention yearbook) FY2022 edition, chapter 不発弾 (UXO) · Okinawa Prefecture, 消防防災年報 FY2024 edition, chapter 不発弾等処理事業の概況
