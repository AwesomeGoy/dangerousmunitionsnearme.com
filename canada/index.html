About this data. This Canada list is compiled from official Department of National Defence UXO records. It isn’t the only source — always check the original agency page and follow its safety guidance.

An inventory of 864 munitions and unexploded-ordnance sites in Canada, compiled from official Government of Canada and National Defence sources. 687 sites have official coordinates and appear on the map, including 687 approximate place-name locations. 177 sites have no published coordinate and are listed in the table only. This site does not geocode names or invent locations. Blank fields mean no source was found.

About “Closed” status: DND’s published “Closed” label does not mean a site is clear of unexploded ordnance. The DND UXO Legacy Sites Program page states that sites with no or low UXO risk may be administratively closed, that administratively closed sites can be re-opened at any time if new information suggests an unacceptable UXO risk, and that no technology available today guarantees all UXO on a site have been located and removed.

Province codes are kept as published, including EC and WC. The DND locations page labels that column Coast. An official expansion of those two codes was not retrieved, so they are not spelled out here.
