What is in this cut

Includes every facility on EPA's current OB/OD list as of the March 11, 2025 table (the same EPA page is also marked July 14, 2026), including Department of Defense, Department of Energy, NASA, contractor, and territorial entries, plus Camp Minden, Savanna Army Depot, and Lake City Army Ammunition Plant, which are not on that current list. JMC conventional demil hubs named in the Oct 1, 2025 Hrycak briefing are flagged when they map to these facilities.

Verification labels

verified: the listed facts were confirmed from official or other allowed primary sources. partly verified: official identity (and usually the EPA listing or a documented incident) is confirmed, but inventory, current operations, or some incident details remain incomplete. unverified: a claim was recorded but not confirmed against a primary source. Blank fields mean no source, not a zero or a no.

Most sites in this first cut are labeled partly verified: the EPA identity, address, permit listing, and FRS coordinates are confirmed, but this draft has not yet built a full incident or inventory record. Sites with a deeper research note still carry gaps, which are stated on the site page.

Coordinates

Latitude and longitude are EPA FRS LATITUDE83/LONGITUDE83 for the site's RCRA ID when available. Collection method and accuracy are recorded when FRS provides them. Coordinates are not guessed or manually offset.

Sources

Preferred sources are EPA and state environmental records, GAO, DoD Inspector General, DTIC, Army and other official releases, court filings, AP, Reuters, and local news. This draft does not cite NYT, CNN, MSNBC/MS NOW, Fox News, Snopes, or PolitiFact.

What is still left out

  • Facilities that are not on EPA's current OB/OD table and were not independently confirmed in this pass as currently operating RCRA OB/OD units. Historical or closed OB/OD units in RCRAInfo are omitted unless separately documented (the three extra sites are Camp Minden, Savanna Army Depot, and Lake City AAP).
  • EPA's Explosive Hazardous Wastes page states that 65 OB/OD facilities were still operating under interim status or a permit as of September 2024, from a cumulative 225 TSDFs that have had OB/OD units. The March 11, 2025 current-list table has 64 rows. This draft did not independently identify the additional currently operating unit, if any, that accounts for that difference.
  • A RCRAInfo public API key was not obtained in this pass, and an ECHO hazardous-waste web-service query was rate-limited (HTTP 429), so no additional currently operating units were confirmed from those systems beyond the official EPA table.
  • Citations still exclude NYT, CNN, MSNBC/MS NOW, Fox News, Snopes, and PolitiFact.

ZIP search

The home-page “Is there one near me?” tool looks up a five-digit U.S. ZIP against a compact table of ZIP Code Tabulation Area internal centroids from the U.S. Census Bureau 2025 ZCTA gazetteer (file dated 2025; INTPTLAT and INTPTLONG, rounded to four decimal degrees). That file is a U.S. government work in the public domain. ZCTAs are generalized representations of ZIP Codes used for statistics; they are not identical to every USPS ZIP Code, and some postal ZIPs have no ZCTA centroid in this table.

Distances are straight-line (great-circle) miles from that ZCTA center, or from a location the browser reports after a “Use my location” click. They are not driving distance and not a measure of risk. The result list is the closest five sites in this draft, plus any other dataset site within 100 miles. The ZIP table is loaded in the browser only when a ZIP is searched. No search or location data is sent to a server.
