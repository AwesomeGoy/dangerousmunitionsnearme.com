What is in this cut

Includes every facility on EPA's current OB/OD list as of the March 11, 2025 table (the same EPA page is also marked July 14, 2026), including Department of Defense, Department of Energy, NASA, contractor, and territorial entries, plus Camp Minden, Savanna Army Depot, and Lake City Army Ammunition Plant, which are not on that current list. JMC conventional demil hubs named in the Oct 1, 2025 Hrycak briefing are flagged when they map to these facilities.

Verification labels

verified: the listed facts were confirmed from official or other allowed primary sources. partly verified: official identity (and usually the EPA listing or a documented incident) is confirmed, but inventory, current operations, or some incident details remain incomplete. unverified: a claim was recorded but not confirmed against a primary source. Blank fields mean no source, not a zero or a no.

Most sites in this first cut are labeled partly verified: the EPA identity, address, permit listing, and FRS coordinates are confirmed, but this draft has not yet built a full incident or inventory record. Sites with a deeper research note still carry gaps, which are stated on the site page.

Coordinates

Latitude and longitude are EPA FRS LATITUDE83/LONGITUDE83 for the site's RCRA ID when available. Collection method and accuracy are recorded when FRS provides them. Coordinates are not guessed or manually offset.

Sources

Preferred sources are EPA and state environmental records, GAO, DoD Inspector General, DTIC, Army and other official releases, court filings, AP, Reuters, and local news. This draft does not cite NYT, CNN, MSNBC/MS NOW, Fox News, Snopes, or PolitiFact.

What is still left out

  • Facilities that are not on EPA's current OB/OD table and were not independently confirmed in this pass as currently operating RCRA OB/OD units. Historical or closed OB/OD units in RCRAInfo are omitted unless separately documented (the three extra sites are Camp Minden, Savanna Army Depot, and Lake City AAP).
  • EPA's Explosive Hazardous Wastes page states that 65 OB/OD facilities were still operating under interim status or a permit as of September 2024, from a cumulative 225 TSDFs that have had OB/OD units. The March 11, 2025 current-list table has 64 rows. This draft did not independently identify the additional currently operating unit, if any, that accounts for that difference.
  • A RCRAInfo public API key was not obtained in this pass, and an ECHO hazardous-waste web-service query was rate-limited (HTTP 429), so no additional currently operating units were confirmed from those systems beyond the official EPA table.
  • Citations still exclude NYT, CNN, MSNBC/MS NOW, Fox News, Snopes, and PolitiFact.

ZIP search

The home-page “Is there one near me?” tool looks up a five-digit U.S. ZIP against a compact table of ZIP Code Tabulation Area internal centroids from the U.S. Census Bureau 2025 ZCTA gazetteer (file dated 2025; INTPTLAT and INTPTLONG, rounded to four decimal degrees). That file is a U.S. government work in the public domain. ZCTAs are generalized representations of ZIP Codes used for statistics; they are not identical to every USPS ZIP Code, and some postal ZIPs have no ZCTA centroid in this table.

Distances are straight-line (great-circle) miles from that ZCTA center, or from a location the browser reports after a “Use my location” click. They are not driving distance and not a measure of risk. The result list is the closest five sites in this draft, plus any other dataset site within 100 miles. The ZIP table is loaded in the browser only when a ZIP is searched. No search or location data is sent to a server.

Map layers (schools and surface water)

Each site page can show a static overlay of public schools and surface water within about 10 miles of the EPA FRS coordinate. The extracts are built at compile time. The browser does not call a live API and no API key is used.

  • Public school points come from the National Center for Education Statistics EDGE Public School Locations, 2023-24 file (download). NCES states that the file is in the public domain. Private schools, child-care sites, and schools opened after that collection are not included. Guam, Hawaii, and the Northern Mariana Islands are in the NCES file when they were reported to the Common Core of Data.
  • Rivers, streams (stream order 3 and higher), and waterbodies of about 4 hectares or more in the conterminous United States come from the EPA Office of Water NHDPlus map service. For Hawaii, Guam, and the Northern Mariana Islands, which are outside NHDPlus coverage, this draft uses the USGS National Hydrography Dataset small-scale territory layers from The National Map. Both are U.S. government works in the public domain. If a territory extract could not be retrieved, the site page says so and the school layer still appears.
  • Public water wells are not mapped. The public USGS NWIS site service returns mixed groundwater inventories (monitoring, domestic, and other wells) without a consistent public-supply use flag in the fields this draft could retrieve, so well locations were omitted rather than shown without that distinction.

Distances in the overlay are straight-line miles from the site coordinate, not driving distance and not a measure of risk. A feature may sit on or just inside a military installation. Presence on the map is not a finding that a school or river is contaminated.

Prevailing wind

Each site page includes a simple wind rose built from a 2016-2025 frequency table at the nearest Iowa Environmental Mesonet ASOS/AWOS station. IEM documents this service at mywindrose.py. The station name and straight-line distance are labeled. Direction is the direction the wind blows from (meteorological convention). This is a multi-year pattern, not a smoke forecast, and wind at the munitions site can differ from the airport station. IEM data-use information is at the IEM disclaimer.

Records request kit and neighbor stories

The records kit builds a letter in the browser from a fill-in form. Nothing is sent or stored. Agency routing links are official FOIA or state public-records pages checked for this draft on November 23, 2026 (America/Phoenix calendar date). Those addresses can change.

The neighbor-stories page explains a planned, moderated submissions feature. The form is disabled and collects nothing. A draft moderation policy and privacy notice are held outside the published site folder for D.A. Williams to review before any collection begins.

Printable fact sheets

Each site has a compact, letter-size fact sheet intended to print on one page. It carries the draft banner in the browser, verification labels on incidents, sources with archived-copy links when a Wayback snapshot was found, and the canonical site URL. It is not an official government document.

Source archive

Where the Internet Archive Wayback Machine availability API already had a snapshot of a source URL, this draft links an "archived copy" next to the live source. If no snapshot was found, a rate-limited Save Page Now request was attempted. This draft does not rehost copyrighted documents. A missing archived-copy link means no snapshot was retrieved, not that the live source is unreliable.

Canada section

The Canada section lists 864 sites from data/canada_sites.json. Pins appear on the map only when the file includes official latitude and longitude. Approximate pins use official Government of Canada place-name coordinates and are labelled as approximate. This draft does not geocode site names. 687 sites are mapped; 177 have no published coordinate and appear in the table only.

See the Canada page for the inventory, filters, and counts.

About “Closed” status: DND’s published “Closed” label does not mean a site is clear of unexploded ordnance. The DND UXO Legacy Sites Program page states that sites with no or low UXO risk may be administratively closed, that administratively closed sites can be re-opened at any time if new information suggests an unacceptable UXO risk, and that no technology available today guarantees all UXO on a site have been located and removed.

Europe section

The Europe page lists 9,737 official EMODnet Human Activities dumped-munitions records (9,459 points and 278 areas). 1,054 records are chemical or mixed chemical and conventional. 8,322 of the point records cite OSPAR ODIMS as the information provider; those are encounter reports, not designated dump sites. Country is derived from Marine Regions EEZ v12 (2023) and is blank for 214 records in high seas or shared waters. Official polygon outlines are kept on 255 of the 278 area records when the ring was reasonably small; the others are shown as centroids and labelled as area centroids. Scotland’s NMPi historic munitions disposal sites layer is not included, because it is not downloadable.

EMODnet Human Activities, Dumped Munitions (CETMAR / EMODnet), CC BY 4.0. Official record: EMODnet metadata · Human Activities portal.

Made with Marine Regions, Flanders Marine Institute (VLIZ), EEZ v12 2023, CC BY 4.0. marineregions.org. Country is derived from those exclusive economic zone polygons and is not an EMODnet attribute. Joint regimes, overlapping claims, and high seas are labelled High seas / shared waters. This page uses a processed compact copy of the official features (polygon centroids, country assignment, and a smaller file for the map and table) and is not endorsed by EMODnet, CETMAR, or VLIZ.

Downloads of the processed page copy: europe-compact.json · europe-sites.csv · europe-geoms.json. The official EMODnet dataset remains the source of record, via the metadata record.
